## ✨ What's New

- Toki supports Claude plans without a 5-hour limit, such as Ultra. A 5-hour indicator in the menu bar shows the weekly limit instead of an empty figure, and account switching and AutoSwap use the weekly limit.
- Each account shows its Claude plan: Pro, Max, Ultra or Team.
- Usage pace: every 5-hour and weekly gauge marks where even spending would be by now and warns when you would run out before the reset. The menu bar adds ↑ to an indicator ahead of pace. You can turn this off in Settings › Menu bar.

## 🛠️ Fixes

- You can drag menu bar indicators in Settings to reorder them again.
